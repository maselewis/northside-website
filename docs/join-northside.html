Join Northside

Interested in joining NSEC?

Interested in helping your community? Working as part of the fire company is a great way to help — and pick up a couple of extras while you’re at it. No experience is needed, and all training is included!

You need to be

  1. At least 18 years old
  2. Hold a valid NJ driver’s license
  3. A resident of the Borough of Tinton Falls, Shrewsbury Township, Red Bank, Lincroft, Shrewsbury or Eatontown for at least 6 months

The rest is easy

  • Complete the application
  • Pass the background checks
  • Receive a favorable recommendation from the investigating committee

You then become a probationary member for 6 months. After that, the active members vote to accept your membership based on your performance and the recommendations of the line officers.

Benefits

Length of Service Award Program (LOSAP)

A statewide program established in 1998 that lets communities provide retirement savings contributions to volunteer emergency responders. Learn more from the State.

Life Insurance

District-supplied life insurance policy once qualified.

Training

Members are trained in fireground operations, safety, ladders, ropes, wildland fire suppression, electrical and chemical hazards, and incident command. Training starts with Firefighter I & II at the Monmouth County, Middletown, or Middlesex County Fire Academy, and continues throughout your career as you progress in experience and command responsibilities.

  • Monmouth County Fire Academy
  • Middletown Fire Academy
  • Middlesex County Fire Academy

Tuition Assistance

The Borough of Tinton Falls provides tuition waivers for volunteer emergency responders up to $600 per year, covering the volunteer, their spouse and dependent children. It starts after your first year of service, with additional credit for each emergency service organization you belong to.

Municipal Fee Waiver

(2-18A.4) Exemption from Tinton Falls Borough fees for recreation activities, training programs, licenses and permits, and use of Borough buildings and facilities, up to $300 per year, for the personal and nonprofit use of members in good standing.

Jury Duty

Active volunteer firefighters may request to be excused from jury duty with verification of active membership. See the state website.
