Join Northside

Northside is looking for volunteers. No experience needed. Training is free. Tell us you’re interested →

Northside Engine Company No. 4 has protected Tinton Falls since 1955, and every member is a volunteer. We answer 300+ calls a year, and we need neighbors who want to learn real skills, make a difference close to home, and be part of a crew that has each other’s backs.

What You Get

Real-world skills

Fire suppression, rescue, ladders, ropes, hazmat awareness, CPR and incident command — skills that help keep you and your family safe.

Free state certification

New Jersey Firefighter I training at the county fire academy at no cost to you.

A second family

Drills, meals, parades, Memorial Day, wetdowns. The friendships are a big reason members stay for decades.

Retirement money (LOSAP)

Tax-deferred contributions toward retirement for active volunteers.

Life insurance

A district-supplied life insurance policy once you qualify.

Tuition and fee waivers

Up to $600 a year in Tinton Falls tuition waivers for you and your family, plus up to $300 in borough fee waivers.

Ways To Serve

Firefighter

Respond to fires, crashes, alarms and rescues. You’ll train to Firefighter I, then keep building skills — driver/operator, officer and specialty training as you gain experience.

Junior member (under 18)

Start learning early. Under New Jersey law, juniors under 16 attend meetings and non-hazardous training; at 16 and up they can join recruit firefighter training with parent permission and close supervision. Juniors never face the same hazards as adult members.

Who Can Join

  1. At least 18 years old (or ask about the junior program)
  2. Hold a valid NJ driver’s license
  3. A resident of the Borough of Tinton Falls, Shrewsbury Township, Red Bank, Lincroft, Shrewsbury or Eatontown for at least 6 months

Firefighting isn’t just for one kind of person. Members come from all walks of life, and we welcome women, people of every background, and people who have never set foot in a firehouse.

How It Works

  1. Tell us you’re interested. Use the form below, call or email. A member will reach out.
  2. Visit Station 4. Meet the members, see the trucks, ask anything.
  3. Apply. Fill out the membership application and pass the background checks.
  4. Investigating committee. A few members get to know you and make a recommendation.
  5. Probation (6 months). Start drilling and responding alongside experienced members.
  6. Firefighter I. Complete New Jersey Firefighter I at the Monmouth County Fire Academy — more than 100 hours of state-certified training at no cost.
  7. Full member. After probation, the active members vote on your membership based on your performance and the line officers’ recommendations.

Common Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. We train you from day one. Many of our members had never fought a fire before joining.

How much time does it take?

Plan on regular drills and meetings, plus calls when you’re available. Firefighter I is the biggest commitment up front: more than 100 hours of classes and drills, usually spread over evenings and weekends. Ask us about our current drill schedule and minimums.

Do I have to answer every call?

No. Nobody makes every call. Members respond when they can, and many with day jobs cover nights and weekends.

Can I join if I work full-time or have kids?

Yes. Many of our members do. Volunteering is built around real lives, and families are part of the firehouse too.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Training at the county fire academy is free, and the district provides your protective gear.

What does Firefighter I training involve?

It’s New Jersey’s state certification course, covering ladders, hose, safety, search and rescue, hazardous materials awareness, incident command and more. You’ll need a physician’s medical evaluation before the academy, and you must be 18 to take part in live-fire training.

What if I’m worried about the physical or emotional side?

That’s a fair question and a common one. You’ll train at your own pace with experienced members, and you won’t be asked to do anything you haven’t been trained for. Firefighters look out for each other, on scene and after. Ask us about the support available.

I don’t live in your area. Where can I volunteer?

Use our fire department finder, or search MakeMeAFirefighter.org, the National Volunteer Fire Council’s national listing of volunteer opportunities.

Únase a Northside — en español

Northside Engine Company No. 4 busca voluntarios. Somos bomberos voluntarios que protegen a Tinton Falls desde 1955. No necesita experiencia: le damos todo el entrenamiento, y es gratis.

Requisitos

  • Tener al menos 18 años (pregúntenos sobre el programa juvenil para menores de 18)
  • Tener una licencia de conducir válida de Nueva Jersey
  • Vivir desde hace al menos 6 meses en Tinton Falls, Shrewsbury Township, Red Bank, Lincroft, Shrewsbury o Eatontown

Lo que recibe

  • Certificación estatal de Bombero I en la academia de bomberos del condado, sin costo
  • Habilidades reales que le ayudan a proteger a su familia
  • Ahorros para la jubilación (LOSAP) y seguro de vida
  • Exención de matrícula de hasta $600 al año para usted y su familia
  • Una segunda familia en la estación

Benefit Details

Length of Service Award Program (LOSAP)

A statewide program established in 1998 that lets communities provide retirement savings contributions to volunteer emergency responders. Learn more from the State.

Life Insurance

District-supplied life insurance policy once qualified.

Training

Members are trained in fireground operations, safety, ladders, ropes, wildland fire suppression, electrical and chemical hazards, and incident command. Training starts with Firefighter I & II at the Monmouth County, Middletown, or Middlesex County Fire Academy, and continues throughout your career as you progress in experience and command responsibilities.

  • Monmouth County Fire Academy
  • Middletown Fire Academy
  • Middlesex County Fire Academy

Tuition Assistance

The Borough of Tinton Falls provides tuition waivers for volunteer emergency responders up to $600 per year, covering the volunteer, their spouse and dependent children. It starts after your first year of service, with additional credit for each emergency service organization you belong to.

Municipal Fee Waiver

(2-18A.4) Exemption from Tinton Falls Borough fees for recreation activities, training programs, licenses and permits, and use of Borough buildings and facilities, up to $300 per year, for the personal and nonprofit use of members in good standing.

Jury Duty

Active volunteer firefighters may request to be excused from jury duty with verification of active membership. See the state website.
