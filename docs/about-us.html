About Northside

Northside Engine Company (NSEC) is a group of volunteers from the community that has been a part of the Borough of Tinton Falls’ fire protection since 1955. Northside is one of the four Tinton Falls fire departments — and the youngest.

We respond to 300+ calls every year no matter the day or time. Our members have on average 15 years of experience, and attend monthly drills and annual training events to maintain the skills necessary to operate the equipment and protect the borough in the event of an emergency. We are all proud to be here, to serve our neighbors when they call.
