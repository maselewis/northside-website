Become a Firefighter

Most fire departments across the country depend on dedicated volunteers from their local communities, and we are no different. If you’re interested in getting involved, we’d love to meet you.

There are many fire departments around the Tinton Falls area, and finding the right one usually starts with finding the closest. Each department is set up and run a little differently, so contacting the one you’re interested in is the best way to learn more.

Find Your Local Fire Department

One important factor when joining is how close the department is to your home or job. No one expects you to make every call, but you need to be able to respond in a reasonable time. Most people choose a department near home; some with flexible jobs respond during the day.
