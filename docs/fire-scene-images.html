Fire Scene Images

Selected images from Northside fire scenes.

2017

From NJ.com: A two-alarm blaze badly damaged a Monmouth County house and drew fire companies from several towns to bring it under control. The fire in the two-story house on Shafto Road near Route 33 was reported around 4 p.m., and firefighters found the house fully engulfed on arrival. No injuries were reported; a backhoe helped demolish part of the structure to expose lingering hot spots.
